2. the factual matrix in which the present civil appeal arises is briefly stated as under 2.1.

ambika thakur was a student who was undertaking an air hostess training program at the frankfinn institute, chandigarh.
2.2. on 10.9.2009, ambika thakur was travelling in a verna car bearing registration number ch04h0297 from chandigarh to bhatinda.
the car met with an accident with a tata ace vehicle bearing registration number pb03t4804 which was being driven in a rash and negligent manner.
