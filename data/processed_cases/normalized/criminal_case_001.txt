HIGH COURT OF KARNATAKA AT BENGALURU
CRIMINAL APPELLATE JURISDICTION

CRIMINAL APPEAL NO. 512 OF 2021

State of Karnataka ... Appellant
Vs
Suresh Kumar ... Respondent

1. The State appeals against the acquittal of the respondent accused of offenses under IPC Section 302.

2. The prosecution relied heavily on circumstantial evidence and eye-witness testimony of PW-1 and PW-2.

3. The trial court rejected the testimony due to minor inconsistencies. Having re-examined the material evidence, we hold that the chain of circumstances is complete and points conclusively to the guilt of the accused.

4. The appeal is allowed and the respondent is granted bail pending sentence determination.
