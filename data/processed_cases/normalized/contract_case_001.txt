HIGH COURT OF BOMBAY
COMMERCIAL DIVISION

SUIT NO. 889 OF 2023

TechSoft Solutions ... Claimant
Vs
Global Logistics Corp ... Respondent

1. This commercial arbitration dispute involves allegations of breach of software services contract and indemnity claims.

2. Clause 14 of the Master Service Agreement specifies that disputes shall be resolved by sole arbitrator under Indian Arbitration Rules.

3. The respondent failed to pay milestone fees amounting to INR 2.5 Crores despite completion of acceptance testing.

4. The tribunal finds clear breach of contract. Claim awarded with interest at 9% per annum.
