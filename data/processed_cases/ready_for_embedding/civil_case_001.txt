IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA
CIVIL APPELLATE JURISDICTION

CIVIL APPEAL NO. 451 OF 2023

BETWEEN:
Ramesh Sharma ... Appellant
VERSUS
State of Maharashtra & Ors. ... Respondents

JUDGMENT

1. This appeal arises out of a civil property dispute concerning land parcel No. 402 in District Nagpur.
The appellant claims possession based on a registered sale deed dated 14th March 1998.

2. The trial court awarded damages and compensation to the appellant. However, the High Court set aside the decree without evaluating the evidence regarding continuous physical possession.

3. We find that the High Court committed a grave legal error in reversing the findings of fact without substantial counter-evidence.

4. Accordingly, the judgment of the High Court is set aside and the order of the trial court awarding compensation is restored.
