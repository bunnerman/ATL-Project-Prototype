HIGH COURT OF DELHI AT NEW DELHI
PROPERTY BENCH

CASE NO: 2022 DHC 1892

Mehta Builders ... Plaintiff
Vs
Ananya Realty Ltd ... Defendant

1. The present suit is filed seeking declaration of title deed and permanent injunction over property measuring 500 sq yards in Saket, New Delhi.

2. The plaintiff produced a registered agreement to sell and partition deed executed in 2015. The defendant claims title through an unregistered power of attorney.

3. It is a settled position of law that an unregistered power of attorney cannot transfer title in immovable property. The defendant failed to establish lawful possession.

4. The suit is decreed in favour of the plaintiff with costs.
